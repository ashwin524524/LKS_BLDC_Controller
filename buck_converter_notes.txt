U3212 auxiliary supply revision — 2026-10-24

Design intent
12 V auxiliary supply for the MCU and gate drivers, with a provisional 150 mA
load budget. This is not a validated 1.5 A supply. Verify startup, ripple, load
steps, component temperatures, and short-circuit recovery across the intended
battery range before applying full motor power. The whole ESC still has other
schematic issues identified in the review; this change does not resolve them.

Circuit
U2 is UNI-SEMIC U3212, C2874740. Pins: 1 VDD, 2 switching reference,
3 FB intentionally open (12 V mode), 4 CS, 5/6 input drain, 8 NC.
Physical lead 7 is absent. No exposed pad.
R13 is 0.5 ohm, 1%, 0.5 W, 1206. Typical 0.55 V CS threshold gives
~1.1 A peak inductor current; 0.50–0.60 V threshold and resistance tolerance
give approximately 0.99–1.21 A before propagation-delay overshoot.
This is a peak current limit, not an available DC output-current rating.
L1 is XR0650-101M: 100 uH +/-20%, 1.5 A thermal rating, 2 A saturation,
595 milliohm DCR. This provides headroom above the selected normal peak limit.
The datasheet minimum off-time limits power at low load/inductance; verify
actual output capability at minimum and maximum input voltage.
C8 is 1 uF, 50 V, X7R between U2 VDD and switching reference.
D3 is M7 with cathode at U2 VDD and anode at the 12 V output.
D2 is SS310, 100 V / 3 A, cathode at switching node, anode at battery return.
C7 is polarized 10 uF / 100 V, 6.3 x 11 mm, 2.5 mm lead spacing.
C9 is polarized 470 uF / 25 V, 8 x 12 mm, 3.5 mm lead spacing.
D1 is a green LED with 4.7 kohm R22 (~2 mA indication current).
FB and NC are explicitly marked unconnected. A power flag documents the
internally started VDD supply for ERC.
Board GND is explicitly joined to -BATT at the supply return. U2 pin 2
must remain on the switching node, not on this board-ground connection.

Libraries and footprints
Custom:U3212 is stored in Custom.kicad_sym and registered in sym-lib-table.
U3212 footprint derives from KiCad SOIC-8 3.9x4.9mm / 1.27mm geometry,
with lead 7 removed, pin 8 retained as NC, and no exposed pad.
XR0650 footprint follows the manufacturer's drawing: 3.7mm inner land gap,
8.4mm outer span, 3.5mm land height; two 2.35 x 3.5mm pads at +/-3.025mm.
C9 footprint matches the selected 12mm height; footprint has no 3D model.
All ten components have Manufacturer, MPN, and LCSC fields in the schematic
and purchasing metadata on the board. buck_converter_bom.csv contains links.

PCB status
Five footprints changed: U2, R13, L1, D2, C9. Original component centers and
orientations were retained as provisional placement. The larger components
need placement review. Removed 51 obsolete buck trace/via objects: private
buck nets and old connections to changed pads. Other routing was preserved;
board GND copper assignments were merged into -BATT to match the schematic.
The board is incomplete and must be rerouted and checked before fabrication.

Verification
KiCad loaded the updated board (44 footprints) and exported all three schematic
sheets. Six expected buck net groups and all 10 PCB component pad/net mappings
were checked against the schematic netlist. Unrelated non-ground routing was
verified unchanged. Supply-sheet ERC has no errors and one intentional warning
for the joined GND/-BATT names; whole-project ERC still has 31 findings
(18 errors, 13 warnings), mostly outside this change. PCB DRC still aborts in
the installed KiCad build, so no successful DRC result is claimed.
