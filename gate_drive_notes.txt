C2/C3/C4: YAGEO CC1206KKX7R9BB475, LCSC C132170.
4.7 uF, 50 V, X7R, +/-10%, 1206. The voltage rating applies across
VB-VS (approximately the 12 V gate supply), not to VB relative to ground.
R7/R8/R9/R10/R11/R12: UNI-ROYAL 0402WGF1002TCE, LCSC C25744.
10 kohm, +/-1%, 62.5 mW, 0402. Retains the existing resistor packages.
R7/R8 changed from 100 kohm; R9-R12 changed from 100 ohm.
All six are gate-to-source pulldowns, including floating high-side sources.

Sizing basis and assumptions
IRLZ44N datasheet gives up to 48 nC total gate charge at Vgs=5 V,
Vds=44 V and Id=25 A. This is NOT the gate charge at our ~12 V drive.
Allow 100 nC per turn-on as a preliminary design budget at the higher drive.
A 10 kohm pulldown draws 1.2 mA at Vgs=12 V and dissipates 14.4 mW.
Allow another 0.2 mA for high-side driver current and leakage in the estimate.
Use C_effective=2 uF as a conservative design assumption for tolerance,
DC bias and temperature, rather than treating nominal 4.7 uF as guaranteed.
This effective-capacitance assumption has not been verified against a
part-specific DC-bias curve and must be checked during hardware validation.
Estimated droop = (100 nC + 1.4 mA * high-side on-time) / C_effective:
  50 us: 0.085 V; 1 ms: 0.75 V.
These are engineering estimates under the stated assumptions, not guaranteed
limits or a simulation. Longer uninterrupted high-side on-times require
re-evaluation; bootstrap operation cannot support indefinite 100% duty.
Firmware must precharge the bootstraps and provide adequate refresh time.
Verify VBS at minimum supply, maximum duty, temperature and motor load.

Why 4.7 uF
A 4.7 uF ceramic provides ample charge margin for normal PWM operation with
the corrected 10 kohm pulldowns, while fitting a practical local 1206 package.
The older LKS reference diagram illustrates 47 uF/25 V; that is an example
application value, not a minimum established for this particular MOSFET/PWM.
The 50 V rating and larger 1206 body help with derating margin; nominal voltage
rating alone does not guarantee a particular effective capacitance.

Layout status
VB-to-VS wiring is unchanged from the previous bootstrap correction.
Updated schematic fields, nine PCB component records, and all three capacitor
footprints. Original component centers/orientations retained provisionally.
Removed five old track/via objects touching capacitor pads whose positions
